### Defects found

- **BUG-12** (P2, Fixed) — A deep link to a section (#p-risks) could land at the top after the app replaced the prerendered page. Root cause: The browser scrolls to the anchor in the prerendered markup; renderApp() then replaces #app, the document collapses for a moment and the position is lost. Nothing re-applied the hash on the first render. Fix: applyInitialHash() scrolls to location.hash once after the first render (instantly). Regression test: tests/journey-regressions.spec.js › BUG-12.
