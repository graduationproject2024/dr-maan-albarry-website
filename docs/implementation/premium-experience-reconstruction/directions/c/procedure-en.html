Cataract & lens surgery

Cataract surgery

A cataract is a gradual clouding of the eye’s natural lens and one of the most common causes of blurred vision with age. Glasses and drops cannot clear it; when it affects your life, the effective treatment is surgery to remove the cloudy lens and replace it with a clear artificial one.

What happens, in plain words

  1. 1

    Eye measurements before surgery to calculate the implant power and discuss the lens type.

  2. 2

    Usually numbing eye drops; you stay awake but do not see the details of the operation.

  3. 3

    A small opening at the edge of the cornea; the cloudy lens is broken up and removed, leaving its capsule.

  4. 4

    A foldable artificial lens is placed inside the capsule.

  5. 5

    Home the same day with a protective shield, eye drops and follow-up instructions.
