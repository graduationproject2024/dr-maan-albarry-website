01Cataract & lens surgery

Cataract surgery

A cataract is a gradual clouding of the eye’s natural lens and one of the most common causes of blurred vision with age. Glasses and drops cannot clear it; when it affects your life, the effective treatment is surgery to remove the cloudy lens and replace it with a clear artificial one.
