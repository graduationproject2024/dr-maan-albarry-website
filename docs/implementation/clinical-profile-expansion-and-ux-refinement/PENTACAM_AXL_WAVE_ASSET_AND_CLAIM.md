# Pentacam AXL Wave — asset and claim

- **Device:** Pentacam® AXL Wave by OCULUS.
  - Launched in 2019 (shown at ESCRS Paris in September 2019).
  - The original Pentacam dates from 2002; the two are not confused anywhere.
- **What it combines:**
  - Scheimpflug tomography;
  - objective refraction;
  - total-eye wavefront analysis;
  - optical biometry;
  - retroillumination.
- **Claim:**
  - Original: «...وهو أول جهاز من نوعه بالمملكة العربية السعودية».
  - Public wording: "Brought the Pentacam AXL Wave into his practice in Madinah".
  - The maker's "first device to combine" is a product claim. It is not evidence of the first Saudi installation.
