# Ocular surface guidance

- **Original:** «من شروط زراعة العدسات الذكية أن يكون سطح العين خاليًا من الأمراض، وعلى رأسها جفاف العين، للخروج بأفضل نتيجة.»
- **How it is presented:** as evaluation and optimisation, not an absolute rule (brief §7).
- **Headline:** «قبل اختيار العدسة الذكية: صحة سطح العين أولًا» / "Before choosing an advanced IOL: ocular surface health matters".
- **Four points:**
  1. why the tear film matters;
  2. dryness affects measurements and the lens-power calculation;
  3. detect, treat, then measure again;
  4. "not a permanent no".
- **Also included:** a seven-item list of what decides the lens.
- **Where it appears:**
  - the full section at `/lens-lab/#ocular-surface`;
  - short notes on `/procedures/cataract-surgery/` and `/procedures/lens-implants/` that link to it.
- **Basis:**
  - ASCRS preoperative ocular-surface algorithm (Starr CE et al., JCRS 2019;45:669-684);
  - AAO EyeSmart IOL page.
- **Status:** pending Dr. Maan's review.
