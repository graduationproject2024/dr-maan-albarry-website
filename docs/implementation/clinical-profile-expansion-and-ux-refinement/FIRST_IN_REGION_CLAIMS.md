# First-in-region claims

- **Search (research run of 2026-11-23):** PubMed, the open web, the Saudi press and society sites.
- **Result:** no independent evidence of priority was found for any "first" claim.
- **Handling:** each claim is published only in a dated, non-comparative form. The original superlative stays in the registry.

| Claim | Public form | Finding | What would unlock the original |
|---|---|---|---|
| First CXL in Madinah, 2010 | CXL in Madinah since 2010 | no evidence | hospital records or press from the time |
| First ICL in Madinah, 2010 | ICL in Madinah since 2010 | no evidence | same |
| First trifocal, EDOF and toric IOLs in Madinah, 2010 | trifocal and toric since 2010 | See notes 1-3 below the table. | owner to clarify the EDOF year |
| First Femto-LASIK in Madinah, 2010 | Femto-LASIK in Madinah since 2010 | no evidence | records |

| First biologics in paediatric uveitis, KSA, 2009 | used from 2009 | no evidence | - |

| First president of the association | **published as before** | See note 4 below the table. | **owner to confirm the organisation and supply a document** |

Notes on the IOL row:
1. EDOF in 2010 is anachronistic: the first EDOF lens (Tecnis Symfony) got its CE mark in June 2014.
2. A trifocal lens in 2010 is plausible: FineVision was marketed from 2010.
3. Toric IOLs were in use from the 1990s. Al-Madina (16 Aug 2016) reports a trifocal implantation by his team, but claims no first.

Note on the association row:
4. The Saudi Society of Cornea, Cataract and Refractive Surgery (SSCRS) was founded by an SCFHS decision on 09/09/2021. Its pages name no president and do not mention him. The owner's word "رابطة" may mean a different, earlier body.

The site makes no claim that he invented any device or technique.
